Privacy Policy — Daigoro SNS Manager

Last updated: 2026-08-27

This Privacy Policy describes how Daigoro SNS Manager ("the Service"), operated by moairo Inc. ("the Company"), handles information in connection with its use of TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. The Service is an internal tool used solely to operate the Company's own official social media accounts and is not offered to the public.

1. Information We Collect and Process

The Service processes: (a) video files and basic file information (such as file size) needed to transfer a video to TikTok; (b) authentication tokens issued by TikTok after an authorized staff member completes TikTok's own login and consent flow; (c) a basic account identifier returned by TikTok (open_id) to confirm that actions are being performed on the correct, Company-owned account.

2. Information Obtained from TikTok

When an authorized staff member connects the Service to the Company's TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, the Service requests the following scopes: user.info.basic (to identify the connected account) and video.upload (to deliver video files to that account's inbox). The Service does not request any other information from TikTok, such as followers, existing videos, or analytics.

3. Purpose of Use

The information above is used exclusively to operate the Company's own official TikTok account: preparing content and sending it to the Company's own TikTok inbox for review prior to publishing. No information is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to this internal operational use.

4. Handling of Video and Other Content

Video files are stored locally on the Company's own equipment before being sent to TikTok. Files sent via the Content Posting API (video.upload) are delivered only to the inbox/draft area of the Company's own, already-authorized TikTok account. The Service does not publish this content automatically; publishing is always performed manually by an authorized staff member within the TikTok app itself.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose the information described in this policy to any third party, except as necessary to communicate with TikTok's own servers as part of the API calls described above, or as required by law.

6. Data Sharing with External Services

Beyond the direct communication with TikTok necessary to deliver video content as described above, the Service does not share data with any other external service or platform.

7. Management of Credentials and Tokens

Authentication credentials (Client Key, Client Secret) and access/refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored exclusively in the macOS Keychain on the Company's own equipment. They are never stored in plain text files, source code, version control systems, or any location outside the Keychain, and are never displayed in logs or shared with any party.

8. Retention / Deletion

Tokens are retained only as long as necessary to operate the Service and are replaced automatically when TikTok issues a refreshed token. An authorized staff member may revoke the Service's access at any time via TikTok's own account settings, or by removing the corresponding credentials from the Keychain.

9. Security

Access to the Service and the equipment on which it runs is limited to authorized Company personnel. Credentials are managed using the operating system's secure credential storage (Keychain) rather than plain configuration files.

10. Changes to This Policy

The Company may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Service or applicable requirements. The current version is always available at this URL.
